Structure types

Built to the lot you actually have.

Coverage is driven by your stall layout, drive aisles and setbacks — not by a catalog kit. These are the configurations we build most.

Free-standing canopies

Columns down the center, coverage spanning both directions. The most efficient way to cover a double row of stalls back to back, and the layout that costs the least per space covered.

Cantilevered single-slope

Columns set behind the stalls so nothing blocks the door swing or the drive aisle. The cleanest look from the street, and what most property managers picture when they ask for covered parking.

Attached & lean-to covers

Tied into an existing wall or structure where the site is tight. Flashed and sealed properly at the building line so you don't trade shade for a leak.

Multi-bay & full-lot runs

Long continuous runs across an entire lot, phased so the property stays usable while we build. Column lines held straight the whole way — the detail everybody notices and nobody mentions until it's wrong.

Scope

What a UFFG canopy includes.

Every proposal is written out line by line, so you can see exactly where your money goes.

  • Site evaluation, field measurement and column layout
  • Coordination of engineered drawings for permit submittal
  • Drilled concrete piers and foundations sized to wind load
  • Steel columns, beams and purlins — fabricated and erected in house
  • Metal roof decking, fascia, flashing and closure trim
  • Gutters and downspouts where drainage calls for them
  • Conduit provisions for canopy lighting when specified
  • Site cleanup, stall re-striping coordination and final walkthrough
