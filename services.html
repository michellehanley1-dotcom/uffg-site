Structure types

Configurations we build.

Coverage is determined by your stall layout, drive aisles and setbacks. These are the configurations we build most often.

Free-standing canopies

Columns down the center with coverage spanning both directions. The most efficient layout for a double row of stalls back to back, and the lowest cost per space covered.

Cantilevered single-slope

Columns set behind the stalls, leaving door swings and drive aisles clear. The most common configuration on commercial properties.

Attached & lean-to covers

Tied into an existing wall or structure where the site is tight. Flashed and sealed at the building line.

Multi-bay & full-lot runs

Continuous runs across an entire lot, phased so the property stays usable during construction. Column lines held to a single survey line the full length.

Scope

What a UFFG canopy includes.

Every proposal is itemized by line.

  • Site evaluation, field measurement and column layout
  • Coordination of engineered drawings for permit submittal
  • Drilled concrete piers and foundations sized to wind load
  • Steel columns, beams and purlins, fabricated and erected in house
  • Metal roof decking, fascia, flashing and closure trim
  • Gutters and downspouts where drainage requires them
  • Conduit provisions for canopy lighting when specified
  • Site cleanup, stall re-striping coordination and final walkthrough

Repairs

Repairs to existing canopies.

Hail and wind events, vehicle strikes and age all take canopies out of service. We assess the existing structure and repair what is sound.

  • Storm and hail damage assessment for insurance claims
  • Struck, bent or corroded column replacement
  • Roof panel and decking replacement
  • Flashing, trim and fastener repair on leaking structures
  • Re-plumbing and re-leveling settled column lines
