Why UFFG

Commercial canopies are all we build.

Canopy work is close-tolerance. A column line can run two hundred feet, and piers set out of position are visible from the far end of the lot. Building covered structures exclusively means our crews measure, set and plumb to that standard on every job.

  • Field measurement and column layout by the crew that erects the steel
  • Drilled piers and foundations sized to the wind load for your county
  • Steel fabricated and erected in house
  • Straight column lines, consistent reveals and finished trim
  • One point of contact from the first site visit through final punch

Process

How a project runs.

Site visit and measurement

We walk the property, measure the area, check drive aisles and overhead clearances, and locate where piers can be drilled.

Layout and proposal

You receive a layout showing column placement and coverage, with a written scope and an itemized price.

Foundations and steel

Piers drilled and poured, columns set and plumbed, beams and purlins framed.

Decking, trim and walkthrough

Roof panels, flashing and closure trim installed last. We walk the finished structure with you before demobilizing.

Tell us what you need covered.

Send the property address, the number of spaces and your timeline. We will schedule a site visit and follow up with a written proposal.
