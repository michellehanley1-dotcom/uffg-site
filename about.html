The company

The business is commercial canopies.

UFFG Construction LLC builds commercial canopies in steel. The company is based in Houston, works across Texas, and has been building covered structures for twenty years.

Most of our work is at apartment communities: covered parking rows, resident walkways and connecting canopies. We also build canopies for office, retail and industrial properties, and tank, equipment and monorail canopies for municipal utility districts across the region.

Canopy work is close-tolerance. Column lines run the length of a parking row, and piers set out of position are visible from the far end. Our crews measure, set and plumb to that standard on every job.

The owner runs the field. He handles site visits and measurements, so the person quoting the job is the person responsible for building it.

Specialties

What we build most.

Apartment parking & walkways

Covered parking rows, resident walkways and connecting canopies for multifamily communities. Phased so residents keep parking during construction.

Commercial canopies

Entry canopies, covered walkways and parking for office, medical, retail and industrial properties.

MUD tank & monorail canopies

Tank covers, chemical and equipment canopies and monorail canopies for municipal utility districts and water treatment sites.

Repairs

Storm and hail damage, struck or bent columns, failed fasteners, leaking flashing and panel replacement.
