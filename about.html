About

Four names on the building.

UFFG stands for Uribe, Fuentes, Franco and Garza — a family name, put on a company that had to earn it.

The company

A steel construction company born from covered structures.

UFFG Construction LLC is a Houston-based commercial contractor built on steel. We started where the tolerances are unforgiving — commercial covered parking, where columns have to land on a line and the roof has to shed water across a hundred feet of lot without a ripple.

That's still the core of what we do, and it's why the rest of the work holds up. A crew that can set a straight column line can hang a canopy over a generator, frame a screen wall, or tie a cover into an existing building without leaving a problem behind.

Ricardo Uribe owns and runs the company. He is on the sites, on the measurements, and on the phone — which is why there's one number to call and one person who already knows your job.

How we work

Four things we don't bend on.

Measure it ourselves

The people who erect the steel are the people who took the measurements. Nothing gets lost between a sales call and a crew.

Price the whole scope

Written line by line. If something isn't included, it says so — before you sign, not after the piers are in.

Keep the site usable

We phase around tenants, residents and traffic, and we clean up every day. The lot still works while we're on it.

Finish the finish

Trim, flashing and closures are not an afterthought. The last ten percent is what the property owner sees for the next twenty years.
